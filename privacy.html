Privacy policy

Helical Spring Designer · last updated 5 November 2026

This policy covers the calculator at yjerv.github.io/spring-calc and the Android app of the same name. They are the same tool. The Android app is a Trusted Web Activity: it opens those pages in Chrome, or in another browser that supports the technology.

Who publishes it

The app is published by YJerv, as an individual hobby developer. Contact is the developer email on the Google Play listing. This page does not publish a postal address. There is no separate account system.

What happens to the numbers you type

The calculator runs on your device. Loads, lengths, wire sizes, material figures and the other values you enter are used to size the spring. They are not sent to YJerv.

The last set of inputs is kept in the browser’s local storage on the device, under the key spring-calc-state-v1, so the design is still there next time. That copy stays on the device. Clearing the app’s storage, or the site data for yjerv.github.io, removes it. On Android, the app’s “manage space” shortcut opens those site settings.

Text, JSON, print and the A3 PDF are produced on the device. They leave the device only if you save or share them yourself.

What this app does not do

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics and no crash-reporting tool run by YJerv.
  • No sale of personal information. YJerv does not hold a store of personal information to sell or share.
  • No in-app purchases. The Android app is a paid download. Google Play takes the payment. YJerv does not receive your card number.

Pages loaded from GitHub

The first time the app opens, and when it refreshes its files, the device requests the calculator from GitHub Pages. GitHub hosts those files and may log the request under GitHub’s privacy statement. YJerv does not operate that logging and does not receive a copy of it.

After the files have been cached, the calculator is built to keep working without a connection.

Google Play

If you install the Android app from Google Play, Google processes the download and, for an ordinary purchase, the payment. That is covered by Google’s privacy policy. A licence tester added in Play Console is not charged. That setting lives in Play Console, not in the app.

Children

This is an engineering calculator. It is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text will be published at this same address and the date at the top will change.
