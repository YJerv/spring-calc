## Full description (4000 characters)

Helical Spring Designer sizes round-wire compression springs in metric units.

Enter the duty as two load–length points, or as one point and a rate. The app works out the rate, the free length and the loads at the working lengths. A working length has to sit inside the range you set, when you have set one.

Application constraints take a pocket, a spring guide, clearances, the working lengths and a service temperature. Leave a box blank when that limit does not apply.

Design options cover the ends, the wind, the quality grade and the material. Presets follow the EN 10270 families used on the page. You can enter the modulus and the tensile strength yourself. Stress is reported in MPa. The allowable check is a screening ceiling, factor times tensile strength, or a stress limit you type in MPa. It is not a certified material approval.

Check searches standard wire sizes and mean coil diameters inside the pocket and the guide. Green means the working deflection is between 30% and 70% of available travel and the corrected stress is within the active limit. Orange means a spring was found only with a wider deflection band, 20% to 80%, or with the stress limit raised by 20%. Red means those allowances are not enough, and the page says what to change.

Optimisation looks for the lowest corrected stress inside the same limits. The wire list keeps standard sizes that have at least one coil diameter that passes. A slider moves the mean diameter, and the page flags coil growth when the compressed outside diameter no longer meets the pocket clearance.

Results show the spring particulars, the BS EN 15800 tolerances for the selected grade, a load–length graph and a scaled side view at free length. The graph marks percent deflection at L1 and L2, and corrected stress on the right-hand axis.

The only export is an A3 PDF drawing. The calculator on screen is free. Until a one-off drawing unlock is switched on, the PDF is a preview: the table names stay and the table values are left out, and the sheet says “Upgrade to release drawing table”. There are no adverts.

The numbers you type stay on the device. The app does not ask you to sign in, and it does not send the design to the publisher.

Standards are named, not copied. You remain responsible for checking a design against the current standard and the real duty.
