Privacy policy

Helical Spring Designer · 18 November 2026

This policy is the draft for the Play listing. It is not published on the live site. It describes the calculator at yjerv.github.io/spring-calc and the Android app that opens those same pages.

Who publishes it

YJerv publishes the app as an individual. There is no account and no sign-in. Contact is the developer email on the Play listing. This page does not give a postal address.

What the app stores

The calculator runs on the device. Loads, lengths, diameters, material figures and the other values you type are used to size the spring. They are kept in the browser’s local storage on that device, under the key spring-calc-state-v1, so the design is still there next time. The colour setting is kept the same way, under spring-calc-theme. YJerv does not receive those values. Clearing the site data, or the app storage, removes them.

The A3 PDF is made on the device. It leaves the device only if you save or share it yourself.

What the code does not do

Checked against the app in this branch:

  • No advertising. The pages do not load an ad network.
  • No analytics and no crash reporting run by YJerv. The app code does not call an analytics service.
  • No account, and no sale or share of personal information. YJerv does not hold a store of personal information.
  • The Play Billing drawing unlock is present in the code and switched off. While it is off, the app does not offer a purchase and does not ask Google Play for a payment.

If the drawing unlock is switched on

A one-off unlock for the complete A3 drawing would be bought through Google Play, using Play Billing. Google handles the payment. The app is written to receive a purchase token, not a card number. YJerv does not receive the card number. Until that product exists in Play Console and the switch is turned on, this purchase is not offered.

Pages loaded from GitHub

The device requests the calculator from GitHub Pages. GitHub hosts those files and may log the request under GitHub’s own privacy statement. YJerv does not operate that log and does not receive a copy of it. After the files are cached, the calculator is built to keep working without a connection.

Children

This is an engineering calculator. It is not directed at children.
